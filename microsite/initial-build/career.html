Early Career

Kahan first toured at the age of 19, and was featured with several other artists. He released his debut album, Busyhead, on December 11, 2018. His album single, Hurt Somebody, became a hit in US charts.

During the onset of the COVID-19 pandemic, Kahan returned to Vernon and finished his second EP, Cape Elizabeth, on May 2020. He returned to Los Angeles in 2020 and released his second full album, I Was / I Am on September 17,2021.
