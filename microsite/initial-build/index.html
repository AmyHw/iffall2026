Noah Berkenkamp Kahan

is an American singer-songwriter. He was born in Strafford, Vermont, and his father taught him how to play guitar as a child. Kahan began writing songs at the age of 8, hosting his first band at the age of 12 and holding his first tour by 19. He had his breakthrough single with Hurt Somebody in 2019. Though most of his early works learned towards indie pop, his later works have shifted towards indie folk and acoustic sounds. His music also draws influences from rock, folk, and pop.
